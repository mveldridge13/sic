1. Your privacy at a glance

We believe sharing expenses shouldn't mean sharing more personal information than necessary.

sic. collects and uses information needed to manage shared expenses, calculate balances and operate the service.

  • What we collect: Your name, email address, account details, group memberships, expenses, payments and, if provided, your PayID and receipt images.
  • Where we store it: Our primary application data is stored in Sydney, Australia.
  • What we don't do: We don't sell or rent your personal information, display advertising, use advertising trackers or access your bank accounts.
  • AI receipt scanning: Receipt images are only sent to Anthropic's Claude AI when you have given permission.
  • Your control: You can manage your personal information, remove your PayID, disable receipt scanning and request account deletion.

sic. is an expense-sharing tool, not a payment service. We calculate and track shared costs but never move money or initiate bank transfers.

4. AI-powered receipt scanning

sic. uses artificial intelligence to extract information from receipts. This functionality is powered by Claude, an AI service provided by Anthropic.

Receipt scanning is optional and requires your permission before any receipt image is sent to Anthropic.

Your consent comes first

The first time you select Scan receipt, sic. explains that your receipt image will be sent to Anthropic for processing.

You can choose to accept or decline. This consent is separate from your account registration and is not required to use sic.

What happens when you scan a receipt?

If you accept:

  1. Your receipt image is uploaded to sic.'s storage.
  2. Our server sends the image to Anthropic for processing.
  3. Claude AI extracts relevant information, including the merchant, date, total and individual items.
  4. The extracted information is returned to sic. and used to populate the expense form.
  5. You can review and edit the extracted information before saving the expense.

Receipt information is processed automatically by AI and is not routinely reviewed by a person.

What information is sent to Anthropic?

Anthropic receives the receipt image through sic.'s server.

We do not intentionally send your name, email address, group details or account information as part of the receipt-scanning request.

Anthropic does not receive your phone's IP address through this server-side request.

However, anything visible in the receipt image is transmitted, including potentially sensitive information such as payment card details or other identifying information.

We recommend photographing only the receipt and avoiding unnecessary personal information in the image.

How Anthropic handles receipt images

Under Anthropic's applicable commercial terms, submitted images are not used to train its AI models.

Anthropic states that submitted data is generally deleted within 30 days, unless it is flagged by automated safety systems or must be retained to comply with legal obligations or enforce its usage policies.

Anthropic may process information in the United States and other countries where it operates.

Reviewing AI-generated information

AI extraction is not always accurate.

You are responsible for checking the extracted amounts, items and other details before saving an expense. sic. does not automatically confirm or settle an expense based on AI-generated information.

If you decline consent

If you decline, your receipt image is not sent to Anthropic and receipt scanning is disabled for your account.

You can continue adding expenses manually.

Changing your consent

You can change your receipt-scanning preference at any time:

Profile → Receipt scanning

  • On: Receipt images can be sent to Anthropic when you scan.
  • Off: Receipt images will not be sent for AI processing.

Turning scanning off does not automatically delete receipt images already attached to saved expenses.

Unsuccessful scans

If a scan fails or you close the expense form without saving, the uploaded receipt image is deleted from sic.'s storage when the form is closed.

5. Managing and deleting receipt images

Receipt images are associated with the expenses to which they are attached.

You can:

  • Remove a receipt image: Open the expense and select Remove photo. The image is deleted while the expense and its financial information remain.
  • Delete an expense: Deleting the expense also deletes its associated receipt image.

Receipt images can be removed by the person who created the expense, the group owner or a co-owner.

Other members who can view the expense can view its receipt image but cannot remove it themselves. They can request removal from an authorised group member or contact us.

7. Guest users and people without an account

Group members can add people to a group before those people register for sic.

These people are referred to as guests.

For a guest, we store:

  • Their name, as entered or selected by a group member.
  • Expenses and payments recorded for them.
  • Their associated group balances.

We do not store their phone number or email address when they are added through the phone contacts feature.

Guest access links

A group member can share a guest's personal view link so that the guest can see their own portion of the group's financial information in a web browser.

The link:

  • Does not require an account.
  • Provides read-only access.
  • Shows only the guest's relevant financial information.
  • Can be deactivated by the group owner, co-owners or the person who shared it.
  • Stops working when the guest joins sic. with an account.

We record when a guest view link was last opened. This activity is visible within the group.

If a guest joins sic., their existing group participation and associated balances become part of their account.

Guests who want their name removed can ask the group owner to remove them or contact us.

8. PayID and payments

sic. helps people keep track of shared expenses and repayments. It does not process payments, hold money, initiate bank transfers or connect to your bank account.

We never request or receive your banking login credentials.

We do not verify payments directly with banks.

A payment counts once the person receiving the money records it or confirms it arrived. A payment recorded by the payer, or by anyone else, waits for the receiver to confirm it. A payment made to a guest, who has no account to confirm it, counts straight away.

PayID information

Adding a PayID is optional.

If you choose to add one, it is visible only to members of groups you own. It is not displayed in groups where you are an ordinary member.

You can remove or update your PayID at any time through Profile.

Your PayID is used to help other members identify where to send a repayment outside sic.

11. How we protect your information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information held by sic. against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

These measures include:

  • Encrypted connections: Connections between the application, website and our servers use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Data encryption: Supabase provides encryption for stored data, including receipt images and backups.
  • Password protection: Passwords are stored in hashed form rather than readable text.
  • Database access controls: Access rules restrict which users can view expenses, payments, PayID information and receipt images.
  • Restricted system access: Access to the systems supporting sic. is limited, with sensitive secret keys kept out of the application.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. We continue to review and improve the safeguards used to protect information.

Data breaches

If a data breach occurs, we will assess it and respond in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Where a breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the relevant regulatory authorities, including the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where required.

Notifications will explain what happened, what information may have been affected and any recommended steps to protect yourself.

12. How long we keep your information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide sic., maintain accurate group records, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes.

Shared financial records

Expenses and payments may remain in a group after a member leaves or deletes their account.

This is necessary because other members rely on those records to maintain accurate balances and settlement histories.

Edit history

When expenses or payments are edited or deleted, relevant historical information may remain in the group's edit history so members can understand previous changes and resolve discrepancies.

Deleted receipt images are removed from active storage.

Technical logs

Technical logs maintained by our hosting providers are retained for limited periods and deleted in accordance with their applicable retention practices.

Backups

Information contained in provider backups may remain temporarily until those backups expire through the provider's normal deletion processes.

We may retain information for longer where required by law or where necessary to resolve disputes, investigate misuse or protect the service.

13. Deleting your account

You can request deletion of your sic. account through the application.

Go to:

Profile → Security → Delete account

You will be asked to confirm your password before proceeding.

Settling outstanding balances

To protect other group members, your account can be deleted once you no longer owe or are owed money in any group.

If a balance cannot be settled or is in dispute, contact us for assistance.

Information deleted immediately

When your account is deleted, we remove:

  • Your login credentials, including email address and password.
  • Active authentication sessions.
  • Your PayID.
  • Your notifications.
  • The groups you've hidden from your list.
  • Your receipt scan count.
  • Your time zone and receipt-scanning preference.
  • Your membership from existing groups.
  • Open invitations you have sent.

Payments to or from you that are still waiting to be confirmed are cancelled, and recurring expenses where you are the payer are stopped.

Group ownership

If you own a group, ownership is transferred to an existing co-owner.

If there is no co-owner, ownership passes to the longest-standing member who has an account.

If no other group member has an account, the group is archived.

Information retained after deletion

Some information must remain available to preserve the financial history of shared groups.

This includes:

  • Your name as displayed in historical group records.
  • Expenses and payments recorded in shared groups.
  • Receipt images still attached to expenses in those groups.

This information remains so that other members' financial records and balances continue to add up correctly.

Your name is not automatically replaced with an anonymous label.

If you want receipt images removed, you must remove them from the relevant expenses before deleting your account.

If you cannot access your account

If you cannot use the application to delete your account, contact us using the privacy contact details above and we will assist.
