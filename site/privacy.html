Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026 · Covers the sic. app and the sic-app.com website

1. Privacy at a glance

sic. helps groups keep track of shared costs. We collect the information we need to run the service, use it to run the service, and handle it responsibly with appropriate safeguards. Our promises:

  • We never sell your personal information or use it for advertising.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • sic. never moves money and never asks for access to your bank.
  • We ask before any receipt photo is sent to AI to be read.

By using sic., you acknowledge that you have read this privacy policy.

2. Who we are

sic. ("we", "us") is the expense-sharing app and website at sic-app.com, operated from Australia. Operator details and a privacy contact email are coming soon.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. Because sic. is available internationally, you may also have rights under the laws where you live (see Your rights and choices).

3. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored in scrambled form, never as readable text).
  • Preferences: such as your currency and time zone.
  • Payment details you choose to add: such as a PayID and account name.

Information created when you use sic. with others

Groups and their members, expenses, items and splits, payments and confirmations, notes, receipt photos, the history of changes, and notifications. Some of this is added by other people in your groups.

Information other people give us about you

A group member can add you to a group by name before you have an account, and include you in expenses and payments (see Guest users). If they pick you from their phone's contacts, only your name is saved; your phone number or email is used on their phone to send you an invite and is not sent to us.

Information collected automatically

Like most online services, our hosting providers record technical information such as IP addresses, device and browser type, and the time of requests. We use it to run the service securely and to investigate problems. We also record how many receipts you scan, to apply the daily scanning limit.

Information on your device

The app stores information on your device (or in your browser) so that you stay signed in and can keep using sic. without a connection. This includes your sign-in session, a copy of the groups, expenses and payments you can see, and anything you add while offline until it is sent.

Signing out removes your sign-in session and the copy of your groups. Anything you added offline that hasn't been sent yet stays on the device until you sign in again and it is sent; it isn't shown to anyone else who signs in on that device.

If you have expenses or payments waiting to sync, reconnect and let them upload before uninstalling the app or clearing its data. Unsynced information may otherwise be lost.

4. How we use your information

  • To provide sic.: recording expenses and payments, working out balances, and showing your groups what they need to see.
  • To communicate with you about your account and activity in your groups, such as notifications and sign-in emails.
  • To keep sic. secure and to prevent, detect and investigate misuse.
  • To provide support when you or someone in your group asks for help.
  • To maintain and improve the service.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

5. AI receipt scanning

Receipt scanning is optional. Before your first scan, we ask whether you agree to receipt photos being sent to Anthropic, whose Claude AI reads them and fills in the expense. If you don't agree, nothing is sent and you can enter expenses yourself. You can change your choice at any time in the app.

The photo is sent from our server, not your device, and we don't send your name, email or group details with it. Anything visible in the photo goes with it, so photograph just the receipt.

Under Anthropic's applicable commercial terms, receipt images are not used to train its AI models. Anthropic generally deletes submitted images within 30 days, subject to exceptions for automated safety reviews and legal requirements.

AI can misread a receipt, so check the details before you save.

6. Who can see your information

sic. is built for sharing with the people in your groups, and what they see depends on their role:

  • Group owners and co-owners can see everything in their group, including every expense, payment, receipt photo and balance.
  • Other members can see expenses they created or are included in, including associated items and receipt photos. They can also see payments they recorded, their own balance and share, and the group total.
  • Everyone in a group sees each member's name.

People who are not members of a group cannot access that group's information through sic..

7. Payments and PayID

sic. is a record of who owes whom. It does not process payments, hold money or connect to your bank, and we never ask for your banking login. Payments are made outside sic. and recorded in it, and we don't verify them with banks.

Adding a PayID is optional. If you add one, it is shown only to members of groups you own, so they can pay you back. You can change or remove it at any time.

8. Guest users

Members can add people to a group by name before they have an account. For a guest, we keep only the name a member entered and the expenses and payments recorded for them.

A member can share a private link with a guest so they can see their own part of the group in a web browser. Guest links provide read-only access to the guest's relevant group information. Anyone with the link can access that information, so it should only be shared with the intended recipient. The link can be deactivated by the group owner, co-owners or the member who shared it. It automatically stops working when the guest joins sic. with an account. We record when a link was last opened, and show this in the group.

When a guest joins with an account, their part of the group becomes part of their account. A guest who wants their name removed can ask the group owner or contact us.

10. Overseas transfers

Our main database is in Australia. Some of our providers process information in other countries, including the United States. We choose providers that protect information with appropriate security and contractual safeguards.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and at rest and access controls that limit who can see what. No online service can be completely secure. Access to personal information by people responsible for operating sic. is restricted to legitimate operational, support, security or legal requirements. If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify you and the regulator as the law requires.

12. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as we need it to provide sic., keep accurate group records and meet legal obligations.

Shared records belong to the whole group. When you leave a group or delete your account, the expenses and payments you were part of stay in the group so everyone's balances still add up, much as an email you sent stays in the recipient's inbox. Information in backups and logs is removed as they expire.

13. Account deletion

You can delete your account in the app once you're settled up in every group.

When you delete your account, your login credentials, PayID, personal settings, notifications and active sessions are removed. Your group memberships are ended, payments to or from you that are still waiting to be confirmed are cancelled, invitations you sent are deactivated and recurring expenses you pay are stopped.

Groups you own are transferred to a co-owner or, if there isn't one, the longest-standing member with an account. Groups without another registered member are archived: they are no longer available in the app, and guest links to them stop working. Their records are kept in line with our data retention practices.

Your name and your past expenses and payments remain in shared groups so other members' records and balances stay accurate. Receipt photos remain attached to their expenses unless you remove them before deleting your account.

If you can't delete your account in the app, contact us and we'll help.

14. Your rights and choices

You can update your details, remove your PayID, turn receipt scanning off and delete your account in the app. You can also ask us to:

  • give you access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete information, where we don't need to keep it;
  • stop optional processing you agreed to, such as receipt scanning.

We may need to confirm your identity first. We aim to respond to privacy requests within 30 days. If a request is complex or requires additional verification, we will let you know. We will not charge a fee for exercising your privacy rights unless permitted by applicable law.

European Union and United Kingdom

If you're in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to restrict or object to processing, to receive your information in a portable form, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We process your information to provide the service you asked for, with your consent for receipt scanning, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

United States

If you live in a US state with privacy laws, such as California, you may have similar rights to access, correct and delete your information. We don't sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights.

15. Children

sic. is for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

16. Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or advertising identifiers. The app keeps information in your browser or on your device only to keep you signed in and to work offline.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as sic. changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change to how we handle your information, we'll let you know before it takes effect.

18. Questions and complaints

Contact us using the privacy email above (coming soon). If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au) or the privacy regulator where you live.
