Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

In short: sic. keeps only what it needs to split costs in a group, stores it in Australia, and never sells it or shows you ads.

  • What we keep: your name, email, currency and time zone; the groups you're in; the expenses and payments you add; your PayID if you add one; and receipt photos you scan.
  • What we don't do: no ads, no selling or renting your information, no tracking or analytics tools, and no access to your bank. sic. never moves money.
  • Receipt scanning is your choice. The first time you tap Scan receipt, sic. asks before any photo is sent to Anthropic to be read. If you decline, scanning stays off and nothing is sent.
  • Who sees what: a group's owner and co-owners see everything in that group; other members see the expenses they added or are part of (including the receipt), their own balance and the group total.

Receipt scanning uses AI

sic. uses artificial intelligence (AI) to read receipts: Claude, an AI made by Anthropic. Receipt photos are sent to Anthropic only if you agree when sic. asks.

  • You're asked first. The first time you tap Scan receipt, sic. explains that the photo will be sent to Anthropic to be read, and asks you to Accept or Decline.
  • If you accept: each photo you scan is sent to Anthropic, where the Claude AI reads the shop, date, total and items and sends them back to fill in the expense. It's read automatically by the AI, not by a person. Only the photo is sent, not your name, email or group.
  • Check what the AI fills in. AI can misread a receipt, so the amounts are yours to check and change before you save. Nothing is decided for you.
  • If you decline: nothing is sent, and receipt scanning is turned off for you. You can still add expenses by typing them in.
  • The photo stays with the expense in sic.'s storage, so people who can see the expense can check it. If you close the form without saving, the photo is deleted.
  • Only photograph the receipt. Anything else in the photo, like a card number or a face, is sent too.

Change your mind any time in Profile → Receipt scanning: On sends photos when you scan, Off stops it. Turning it off doesn't delete photos already saved with expenses; remove those as below.

Deleting a receipt photo

  • Keep the expense, delete the photo: open the expense and tap Remove photo. The photo is deleted for good and the expense stays as it is.
  • Delete the expense: its receipt photo is deleted for good along with it.
  • Who can do it: the person who added the expense, and the group's owner and co-owners. Other people in the split can see the photo but can't remove it; ask one of them.

Keeping and deleting, and your choices

Shared records stay as long as the group needs them to keep balances right. You can delete your own account at any time.

  • Deleted expenses and payments disappear from the group, but a copy of the details stays in the edit history so balances can be checked and mistakes undone. A deleted expense's receipt photo is deleted for good.
  • Leaving a group keeps your past expenses and payments in it, because the other members' balances depend on them. Your notifications stay too.
  • Deleting your account: in the app, go to Profile → Security → Delete account. You'll need to be settled up in every group first, so nobody is left out of pocket. Your login, PayID, settings and notifications are deleted, and groups you own pass to another member. Your name stays on shared expenses and payments so each group's history still adds up, and so do receipt photos attached to them. To delete those too, remove them from the expenses before you delete your account.
  • Your choices: decline receipt scanning; remove your PayID in Profile; leave any group; turn off sic.'s access to your camera, photos or contacts in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Your rights: under Australian privacy law you can ask to see the information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Contact us and we'll reply within 30 days.
