Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026 · Covers the sic. app and the sic-app.com website

In short: sic. keeps only what it needs to split costs in a group, stores it in Australia, and never sells it or shows you ads.

  • What we keep: your name, email, currency and time zone; the groups you're in; the expenses and payments you add; your PayID if you add one; and receipt photos you scan.
  • What we don't do: no ads, no selling or renting your information, no analytics or tracking tools, and no access to your bank. sic. never moves money.
  • AI receipt scanning is your choice. The first time you tap Scan receipt, sic. asks before any photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to be read. If you decline, nothing is sent.
  • Who sees what: a group's owner and co-owners see everything in that group; other members see the expenses they added or are part of (including the receipt), their own balance and the group total.

Receipt scanning uses AI

sic. uses artificial intelligence (AI) to read receipts: Claude, an AI made by Anthropic. Receipt photos are sent to Anthropic only if you agree when sic. asks.

  • You're asked first. The first time you tap Scan receipt, sic. explains that the photo will be sent to Anthropic to be read, and asks you to Accept or Decline. This is a separate choice, not part of signing up.
  • If you accept: the photo is saved to sic.'s storage, then sic.'s server sends it to Anthropic, where the Claude AI reads the shop, date, total and items and sends them back to fill in the expense. It's read automatically by the AI, not by a person.
  • What Anthropic receives: the receipt image, sent from sic.'s server rather than from your phone. We don't send your name, email, group or account details, and Anthropic doesn't receive your phone's IP address. Anything visible in the photo, like a card number or a face, is sent too, so photograph just the receipt.
  • What Anthropic does with it: under Anthropic's commercial terms it doesn't use the images to train its AI. Anthropic says it deletes them within 30 days, unless its automated safety systems flag the request (then it can keep it for longer to enforce its usage policy) or the law requires otherwise.
  • Check what the AI fills in. AI can misread a receipt, so the amounts are yours to check and change before you save. Nothing is decided for you.
  • If you decline: nothing is sent, and receipt scanning is turned off for you. You can still add expenses by typing them in.
  • If a scan fails or you don't save: the photo is deleted from sic.'s storage when you close the form without saving.

Change your mind any time in Profile → Receipt scanning: On sends photos when you scan, Off stops it. Turning it off doesn't delete photos already saved with expenses; remove those as below.

Deleting a receipt photo

  • Keep the expense, delete the photo: open the expense and tap Remove photo. The photo is deleted for good and the expense stays as it is.
  • Delete the expense: its receipt photo is deleted for good along with it.
  • Who can do it: the person who added the expense, and the group's owner and co-owners. Other people in the split can see the photo but can't remove it; ask one of them, or contact us.

PayID and money

  • sic. tracks shared costs. It doesn't move money, process payments or start bank transfers.
  • sic. never asks for or receives your bank login details, and has no access to your bank account.
  • sic. doesn't check with any bank whether a payment was made. A payment counts once the person receiving it confirms it in the app (or records it themselves).
  • Adding a PayID is optional. It's shown only to members of groups you own, never in groups where you're a member, and you can remove it in Profile at any time.

People who don't use sic.

Members can add someone to a group by name, as a guest, before that person has an account. For a guest we keep only the name a member typed or picked, and the expenses and payments recorded for them. Their phone number or email is never stored (see Your phone's contacts above). When a guest joins with an invite link, their share becomes part of their own account. If you're a guest and want your name removed, ask the group's owner to remove you, or contact us.

Information that leaves Australia

Your sic. data is stored in Sydney. Some processing happens elsewhere:

  • Receipt scanning: accepted receipt photos are sent to Anthropic in the United States, and may be processed in other countries where Anthropic operates.
  • Server steps and support: our providers' servers that run parts of sic. (such as the receipt-scanning step) usually run in the region nearest you, and their support and security staff may access systems from other countries when needed.
  • Website: visits to sic-app.com are served from Vercel's worldwide network.

We choose providers that protect information with security and confidentiality commitments. If you're outside Australia, your information is transferred to Australia (and the countries above) to provide sic. to you.

How we protect your information

  • All connections between the app, the website and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Our database host, Supabase, encrypts stored data, including receipt photos and backups.
  • Passwords are stored only in hashed form.
  • Database access rules decide who can see each expense, payment, PayID and photo, so other users can't reach information the app doesn't show them.
  • Access to the systems behind sic. is limited to the people who run it, with secret keys kept out of the app.

If something goes wrong: if a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we'll tell the people affected and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (and any other regulator where the law requires it) as soon as we can, and explain what happened and what to do.

Keeping and deleting

Shared records stay as long as the group needs them to keep balances right. Your own account can be deleted at any time.

  • Deleted expenses and payments disappear from the group, but a copy of the details stays in the edit history so balances can be checked and mistakes undone. A deleted expense's receipt photo is deleted for good.
  • Leaving a group keeps your past expenses and payments in it, because the other members' balances depend on them. Your notifications stay too.
  • Technical logs kept by our hosting providers are kept only for a short time, then deleted automatically.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Security → Delete account and confirm with your password. It takes effect straight away.

  • Settle up first. You can delete once you don't owe and aren't owed money in any group, so nobody is left out of pocket. If a balance can't be settled or is in dispute, contact us and we'll help work it out.
  • Deleted straight away: your login (email, password and sessions), PayID, notifications, groups you've put away, scan count, time zone and receipt-scanning choice. You're removed from every group, payments waiting on you are cancelled, your open invites stop working and recurring expenses you pay are stopped.
  • Groups you own pass to a co-owner, or to the longest-standing member with an account. A group with nobody else who has an account is closed.
  • What stays, and why: your name, and the expenses, payments and receipt photos in shared groups, stay so the other members' history and balances still add up. Your name isn't replaced with an anonymous label. To delete receipt photos too, remove them from the expenses before you delete your account.
  • Backups: copies in our hosting provider's backups are deleted automatically as those backups expire.
  • Can't use the app? Contact us and we'll delete your account for you.
  • We may keep information longer if the law requires it or it's needed to resolve a dispute or investigate misuse.

Your rights

  • See and correct: you can change your name, currency and PayID in Profile, and ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you (account details, groups, expenses, payments, receipt photos and PayID) or to correct it.
  • Delete: delete your account in the app, or ask us to.
  • Withdraw consent: turn receipt scanning off in Profile; turn off sic.'s access to your camera, photos or contacts in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Leave: leave any group, or remove your PayID, whenever you like.

We'll reply to requests within 30 days, and won't charge you for them. We may need to confirm it's really you first.

If you're in the European Union, the UK or a similar region, you also have the right to ask us to restrict or stop processing, to object to it, and to receive your data in a portable format. We process your information to provide the service you signed up for, with your consent for receipt scanning (which you can withdraw at any time), and for our legitimate interest in keeping sic. secure. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

If you're in California or another US state with privacy laws, we don't sell or share your personal information for advertising, and you can ask to know, correct or delete the information we hold. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Children

sic. is for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we'll delete it. If you think a child is using sic., please contact us.

Cookies and tracking

The sic-app.com website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and doesn't load anything from other companies. The app doesn't use advertising IDs or tracking tools. Our hosting providers keep the technical logs described above.

Complaints

Please contact us first and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), or to the privacy regulator where you live.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, sic. will tell you in the app before the change applies, and update the date at the top. Sending receipt photos anywhere new would mean asking you again.
