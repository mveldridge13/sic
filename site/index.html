How it works

Three steps. No spreadsheets.

Start a group, add what you spend as you go, and let sic. work out the simplest way to square up.

Start a group

A trip, your house, a dinner club. Invite people with a link, or just add their name and they can join later.

Add expenses

Split equally, by amounts, by percentage or item by item. Snap a receipt and sic. reads it for you.

Settle up

See exactly who owes who, pay back with PayID, and the other person confirms it landed.
