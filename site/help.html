👋 Getting started

What is sic.?

sic. keeps track of who paid for what in a group, like a trip, a share house or a dinner club. Everyone can see where they stand, and sic. works out the simplest way to settle up.

sic. never moves money. You pay people back however you like (bank transfer, PayID, cash) and record it so the balances stay right.

The phone apps are coming soon to Google Play and the App Store. You can use sic. in your web browser today.

How do I create an account?
  1. Go to sic-app.com/app/sign-up (or tap Sign up at the top of this page).
  2. Enter your name (this is how friends will see you), your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
  3. Tap Create account.

If someone sent you an invite link, open it first. sic. remembers the invite and adds you to the group once you've signed up.

How do I create my first group?

Go to the Groups tab and tap New group. It takes three quick steps:

  1. What's it for? Pick Trip, Home, Housemates, Family, Dining or Other. This sets the group's emoji.
  2. Who's in? Tap people you already share a group with, or tap Someone new and type their name. Tap That's everyone when you're done.
  3. Call it… Type a name or keep the suggestion. The group uses your usual currency; tap Change to pick another.

Tap Create, then add the first expense or open the group.

👥 Groups & people

How do I invite people to a group?

Open the group, tap the ⋯ button at the top right and choose Share an invite link. Send the link however you like. When they open it, they sign up (or log in) and join the group.

They can also go to Groups, tap Join group and paste the invite code or link.

You don't have to wait for people to join. Add them by name now (see the next question) and start adding expenses straight away.

Can people join without creating an account?

Yes. Anyone can be added to a group by name, as a guest. Guests don't need the app, and you can include them in expenses and payments like anyone else. They show with a dashed outline in the group.

Their own view link. Tap a guest's face in the group and use Share [name]'s link. It opens a private page in their web browser showing their balance, who they pay or get paid by, the expenses they're part of, and the owner's PayID if they owe the owner. It's view only. The card shows when the link was last opened, and you can Turn off link at any time.

Joining later. When a guest opens an invite link and signs up, sic. asks "Are you one of these people?". They pick their name and keep their whole history.

Who can see what in a group?
RoleWhat they see
OwnerEverything in the group: every expense, payment, receipt and balance
Co-ownerThe same as the owner (the owner chooses co-owners)
MemberThe expenses they added or are part of, their own balance and share, and the group total
GuestNothing, unless someone shares their view link (see above)

The owner can make someone a co-owner, hand the group over or remove someone: tap that person's face in the group.

How do I leave, close, hide or delete a group?

All of these are in the group's ⋯ menu.

  • Close group (owner and co-owners): ends the group for everyone once everyone's square and no payment is waiting to be confirmed. Nothing in a closed group can change. Reopen group undoes it.
  • Hide from my list: puts the group away just for you. It comes back by itself when someone adds a new expense.
  • Leave group (members): once you're settled up.
  • Delete group (owner only).

🧾 Expenses & splitting

How do I add an expense?
  1. Tap Add expense on Home or on a group's page.
  2. Type the amount and what it was for, e.g. "Pizza".
  3. The line under the amount says who paid and how it's split ("Paid by you · Split equally between 3"). Tap it to change either.
  4. Tap Save. Everyone's balances update straight away.

From Home you can tap the group at the top to pick a different one.

What are the ways to split?
SplitHow it worksGood for
EquallyEveryone ticked pays the same. Untick anyone who wasn't in it.Most shared costs
AmountsType what each person pays. A line shows what's left until it adds up.One thing paid in different portions
PercentEach person's percentage; must add up to 100%.Agreed splits, like 60/40 rent
SharesSomeone with 2 shares pays twice as much as someone with 1.Couples or families
ItemsAdd lines for each thing and tap who had it.Restaurant bills and shopping

Every cent is accounted for: when a total doesn't divide evenly, the leftover cents go to some people so the shares always add up exactly.

How do I split a bill by item, with tax and tip?

Choose Items in the split panel and add a line for each dish or product, then tap who had it. A line shared by several people is split evenly between them.

Whatever the lines don't cover (the bill total minus the items) shows as Everything else. That's usually tax, tip or a service charge. Choose:

  • In proportion: whoever had more pays more. Best for a tip.
  • Split equally: shared evenly between everyone ticked.

If the lines add up to more than the total, sic. says by how much and won't save until it's fixed.

How do saved splits work?

If your group splits some costs the same way every time (say electricity 50/25/25), save it once and pick it with one tap.

  • Save one: set up an Equally, Percent or Shares split, tap Save this split for later and name it. (Amounts and Items only fit one bill, so they can't be saved.)
  • Use one: saved splits show as buttons at the top of the split panel.
  • Default: the owner or a co-owner can make one the group's default, so every new expense starts with it.
  • Manage: the group's ⋯ menu → Saved splits.

Editing or deleting a saved split never changes past expenses.

What are categories?

Every expense has a category, like Food & drink, Groceries, Transport or Bills & utilities. sic. suggests one from what you type (for example "Uber to the airport" → Transport). Tap the Category row to pick a different one.

In Activity, use All categories to see only one kind of spending.

How do I edit or delete an expense?

Tap the expense to open it, then tap Edit or Delete. When you edit, the old version is kept in the expense's history so everyone can see what changed. Balances update straight away.

Expenses in a closed group can't be changed until the group is reopened.

📷 Receipts

How do I scan a receipt?
  1. On a new expense, tap Scan receipt under the amount, then take a photo or choose one.
  2. Keep the whole receipt in the picture, flat and in good light.
  3. The shop name, total and each item fill in, and the split opens on Items. Check them, tap who had each item and save.

Always check before you save: a blurry or crumpled receipt can be misread. You can scan up to 20 receipts a day.

Where does my receipt photo go?

The first time you scan, sic. asks before any photo is sent to be read by Anthropic's Claude AI. Only the photo is sent, not your name or your group. If you decline, nothing is sent and you can type expenses in as usual. You can change this any time in Profile → Receipt scanning.

The photo is kept with the expense. Anyone who can see the expense can see it. To delete it, open the expense and tap Remove photo.

🤝 Balances & paying back

How does sic. work out who owes who?

Each expense adds to what people owe the person who paid, based on the split. sic. then adds everything up and works out the fewest payments to get everyone square. You'll see it as arrows under Who owes whom on the group's page.

Tap a person's face to see every expense and payment between the two of you, and what each one added.

How do I pay someone back?
  1. Tap Pay back on a "You owe" card (on Home under To settle, or on the group's page).
  2. The full amount is filled in. Tap Pay a different amount to pay part of it.
  3. Pay them in your banking app, by cash or however you agreed. If they've added a PayID, it's shown with Copy buttons.
  4. Slide the button to record that you've paid. They're asked to confirm it arrived.
Why is my payment "waiting to confirm"?

A payment only counts once the person who received the money agrees it arrived. Until then your balance doesn't change, and the payment shows with ⏳.

Who records itWhat happens
The person receiving the moneyCounts straight away
Anyone paying a guestCounts straight away
The payer, or someone elseWaits for the receiver to confirm

The receiver sees "says they paid you · did it arrive?" on Home and taps Confirm or Not received. It never confirms itself. You can tap the waiting payment to remind them (once a day) or cancel it.

How do I fix or undo a payment?

Tap the payment to open it. Correct changes the amount or note (the old one is kept in the history), and Undo removes a payment recorded by mistake. A new amount entered by anyone but the receiver needs confirming again.

What is PayID and who can see mine?

PayID lets people pay you from their Australian banking app with your mobile number, email or ABN instead of bank details. Add yours in Profile → PayID (shown when your currency is AUD).

It's only shown to members of Australian-dollar groups you own, never in groups where you're just a member. You can remove it at any time.

How do I remind someone to pay?

Owners and co-owners see a Nudge button on "owes you" cards and a Remind button under anyone who owes. It sends them a notification, once a day at most.

🔁 Recurring bills

How do I set up a recurring expense?
  1. Add an expense as usual, then tap Recurring under "Paid by".
  2. Pick Weekly, Fortnightly or Monthly, and choose the first date.
  3. Set when it ends (never, on a date, or after a number of times) and a heads-up (same day up to a week before).
  4. For bills like power, switch on Amount changes each time.

Fixed amounts are added automatically on each date and everyone in them is told. For amounts that change, a card asks for this time's amount on the date.

How do I pause, change or stop one?

On the group's page, tap Recurring (or See all) and open the one you want. You can Pause, Edit (changes future ones only, including the next date) or Stop it. Expenses already added stay as they are.

Recurring bills are visible to the group owner, co-owners and whoever set them up.

💱 Currencies

How do I add an expense paid in another currency?

Every group settles up in its own currency. If you paid in something else, tap the currency symbol next to the amount (for example A$ ▾), pick what you paid in and type the amount from the receipt. There are 30 currencies to choose from.

sic. shows what it comes to in the group's currency, using the European Central Bank's daily rate. Tap that line to type your own rate or the exact amount your bank charged.

How does currency conversion work?
  • The rate is locked when you save. If rates move later, the expense doesn't change.
  • Balances stay in the group's currency, so nobody has to convert anything to settle up.
  • What you actually paid is kept and shown on the expense, e.g. "Paid JP¥10,000".

Recurring expenses are always in the group's currency.

⚙️ Account & settings

How do I change my name, currency or password?

Go to Profile and tap the row you want to change: Name, Default currency or Change password. Your default currency only applies to new groups; existing groups keep theirs.

What notifications will I get?

Tap the bell on Home to see them. You're told when someone adds you to a group, reminds you to settle up, says they paid you, confirms or rejects your payment, makes you a co-owner or owner, and when a recurring bill is coming up or has been added.

Notifications on your phone's lock screen are coming soon.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Security → Delete account and enter your password to confirm.

  • You need to be settled up in every group first, so nobody is left out of pocket.
  • Groups you own pass to a co-owner, or otherwise to the longest-standing member.
  • Your name stays on past expenses so the group's history still makes sense.
Is my information private?

Yes. sic. keeps only what it needs to split costs, stores it in Australia, and never sells it or shows you ads. It never moves money or asks for your bank login. Read the full privacy policy.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

Can I use sic. without signal?

Yes, for the basics. Your groups and balances are kept on your phone, and new expenses and payments you add without signal are saved and marked Waiting to sync. They're sent as soon as you're back online.

Editing existing items, recurring bills, scanning receipts and invites need a connection.

Sign up takes me straight into the app

That means you're already logged in on this browser, so sic. takes you to your groups. To create a different account, log out first (Profile → Sign out) or use a private browser window.

I paid someone but my balance hasn't changed

The payment is probably waiting for them to confirm it arrived. See Why is my payment "waiting to confirm"?. You can send them a reminder from the payment.

I can't add an expense to a group

The group has probably been closed. A closed group says when it was closed and by whom, and nothing in it can change. The owner or a co-owner can tap Reopen group in the ⋯ menu.

I can't leave a group or delete my account

You need to be settled up first. Check To settle on Home: pay back what you owe, and make sure any payments waiting to be confirmed have been confirmed.

No answers match . Try a different word, like "split", "PayID" or "guest".
